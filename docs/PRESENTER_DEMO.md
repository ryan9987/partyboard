# One-phone presentation

1. Open `/host` on the shared screen. Use the deployed HTTPS site on the phone.
2. Scan its QR code, join with a name and unused color, and tap **Enable motion**.
3. On the host, click **Demo mode**. Hazel, Moss and Pip fill the party; their turns are automatic.
4. Click **Start game**. Hand over the phone, screen facing the visitor, with a secure grip.
5. Two small shakes roll. On their turn they can use one item before rolling. Shops wait for
   the visitor to choose; bots shop automatically. Each full rotation starts a round game.
6. Follow the animated instructions: tilt to balance/aim, flick to cast/pass/strike, lift to
   reel. Archery now releases with a wrist flick. The phone's Touch fallback is available
   if the venue device has no usable sensors.
7. Results announce the real winner and return to the trail. First to space 47 wins the match.

Demo opponents are deliberately novice: slow reactions, inaccurate shots, poor balance and
conservative item choices. They do not alter visitors' dice or scores; visitor wins are not
guaranteed. Full multiplayer supports eight real players; demo mode is optional.

## Final physical rehearsal (not replaceable with desktop automation)

- Test the actual phone on HTTPS: enable motion, two-shake roll, left/right tilt, settled-wrist
  flicks, recenter, and return from a refresh. iOS Shake to Undo is an OS setting the page
  cannot suppress; turn it off in Accessibility → Touch if needed, or use the touch fallback.
- Keep the phone awake, charged and on the same reliable network. Warm the hosted service
  before judging. Open the host and phone after deployment to use matching protocol versions.
- Confirm laptop frame rate with eight characters if presenting eight-player footage.
- Try every mini-game once. Bots do not require additional phones or accounts.
